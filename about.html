Our Story

A language and nursing school built for Khemisset

EuroPass started with one belief: a class that ignores your actual goal is a class that wastes your time — wherever you're starting from in Khemisset.

Mission

Accompanying every student in Khemisset toward a goal that matters to them

We support students, professionals, and families in Khemisset in achieving their personal and professional goals through practical, results-oriented German, French, English, and nursing training.

Vision

The definitive language and nursing school in Khemisset

Our first chapter is becoming the local reference in Khemisset for language training and nursing education. Our next chapter extends that same standard, in person, as our programs grow.

The Full Picture

Everything EuroPass Actually Is

Who We Are

EuroPass is a language and nursing training center based at Al-Atiq Offices in Khemisset, Morocco. We're not a franchise or a call center forwarding you to someone else — we're a real, single campus where you walk in, meet the person teaching you, and stay with that same team from your first lesson to your last. We started as an English school and grew, program by program, into what we are now: a place that teaches German, French, and English, trains nursing students, and helps German-track students actually get placed in Ausbildung contracts.

What We Offer

Four language and career paths, each built around a specific outcome rather than a generic textbook: German (in person or online, A1 through B2, built for Ausbildung), French (DELF-aligned, in person or online), English (Business, Kids, Communication, IELTS/TOEFL prep, and a fully online track), and Nursing (structured clinical fundamentals and hands-on practice, with no German or migration plan required). For students who want to work in Germany specifically, our separate Ausbildung Training & Placement track pairs German language study with real introductions to partner employers. Every path starts the same way — a free level test, in German, French, or English, that takes a few minutes and tells you exactly where to begin.

Why Khemisset

We built EuroPass here on purpose. Khemisset doesn't have a shortage of people who want to learn a language or start a career in healthcare — it has a shortage of schools willing to be local, accountable, and easy to actually visit. Our campus at Al-Atiq Offices, on Hassan I Avenue near Al-Atiq Mosque, is a real address you can walk into, not a virtual office forwarding calls somewhere else.

Real Teachers, Named and Known

Every program on this site is taught by someone you can name, not an anonymous rotating staff. Classes stay small on purpose, because a language you're too shy to speak out loud in front of thirty people is a language you won't actually learn. We'd rather tell you honestly how you're progressing than tell you everything is going great when it isn't — that's true whether you're a student in the classroom or a parent asking about your child's progress.

Where We're Headed

Our first goal was simple: become the school people in Khemisset actually recommend to each other. That's still the goal. As our programs grow — more languages, more Ausbildung placements, more nursing graduates — we're committed to staying exactly this size of personal: a place where the person who tests your level is often the same person who'll be teaching you months later.
