Curriculum

What you'll cover

Anatomy basics, patient safety, hygiene protocols, and core nursing terminology.
Supervised clinical practice: vital signs, basic procedures, and patient communication.
Introduction to pediatric, geriatric, and emergency care fundamentals.
Final clinical assessment and preparation for your nursing certificate.

Why This Program

Why Nursing Is a Career Worth Training For Properly

Why Nursing Is a Career, Not Just a Job

Nursing is one of the few fields where the work itself still matters every single day — you’re not filing paperwork nobody reads, you’re helping someone through one of their harder moments. That kind of work deserves real training, not a shortcut certificate that leaves you unprepared for your first shift.

What You’ll Actually Be Able to Do

By the end of this program, you won’t just know nursing theory — you’ll have practiced the real, hands-on skills: taking vital signs, following hygiene and safety protocols, communicating clearly with patients who are scared or in pain. Those are the skills that actually get used on day one of a real job.

How We Teach Real Clinical Skills

Classroom learning is only half the program. The other half is supervised clinical practice, where you work through real procedures under guidance before you’re ever doing them alone. Mistakes happen in practice, with feedback, not for the first time on a real patient.

Why Learn With EuroPass

Nadia has spent her career as a working nurse before moving into teaching, so what you’re learning reflects how hospitals and clinics actually operate, not just a textbook version of the job. And if you later decide you want to work in Germany specifically, our separate Ausbildung Training & Placement track is there — entirely optional, never assumed.
