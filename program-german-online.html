Why This Program

Why Online German Can Still Get You to B2

Why Online Doesn’t Mean a Watered-Down Version

The biggest myth about online language learning is that it’s automatically less effective than sitting in a classroom. What actually determines your progress is live speaking practice and real feedback — and you get both here, on a schedule that fits an actual working life instead of fighting against it.

What Learning German Online Actually Feels Like

Your two weekly live classes work exactly like an in-person session — real conversation, real corrections, a real teacher watching your progress. The difference is you’re joining from wherever you are, and the self-paced lessons between classes mean you’re never stuck waiting a full week to review something that didn’t click.

How We Keep You Accountable From a Distance

Monthly one-on-one check-ins with Anna make sure nothing slips through the cracks the way it can with a purely self-paced course. You’ll always know exactly where you stand and what to focus on next, the same as if you were walking into her classroom every week.

Why Learn With EuroPass

Anna designed this program specifically for people balancing work or family alongside German study — she knows the flexible format only works if the teaching quality doesn’t drop, so it doesn’t. Same curriculum, same outcomes, as our in-person track.
