Why This Program

Why Flexible Doesn’t Have to Mean Less Effective

Why Flexibility Doesn’t Mean Less Progress

A lot of people assume online means watered-down — pre-recorded videos, no real feedback, no real teacher. That’s not how this works. You get live video classes with a real teacher, twice a week, plus a library of lessons you can revisit whenever your schedule actually allows it. The format changes; the standard doesn’t.

What a Week Actually Looks Like

Two live video sessions give you real speaking practice and direct feedback, the same as sitting in a classroom. Between sessions, you work through short video lessons on your own time — during a lunch break, after the kids are asleep, whenever it fits. Nothing waits for a fixed weekly slot except the two live classes.

How We Keep Online Learning Real

Live classes stay small on purpose, so you’re not one face among fifty on a call — you’ll actually get called on, actually get corrected, actually get known by your teacher. Monthly one-on-one check-ins make sure the online format never turns into learning alone.

Why Learn With EuroPass

Nora built this program specifically for people juggling work, family, or both — she understands that “I don’t have time” is usually the real barrier, not motivation. The self-paced library exists because we’d rather you learn consistently in fifteen-minute pieces than not at all.
