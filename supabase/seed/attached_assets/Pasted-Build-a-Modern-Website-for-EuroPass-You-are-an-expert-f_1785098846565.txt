Build a Modern Website for EuroPass

You are an expert full-stack developer, UI/UX designer, SEO specialist, and performance engineer.

Build a complete production-ready website for EuroPass, a language school and professional training center in Khemisset, Morocco.

Main Goals

The website should:

- Generate local leads.
- Rank highly on Google and AI search engines.
- Be extremely fast (90+ Lighthouse scores).
- Look modern and premium.
- Be easy for non-technical staff to manage.
- Be fully responsive.
- Support English, French, Arabic, and later German.
