Why This Program

Why Ausbildung Is Different From Just Learning German

Why Ausbildung Is More Than a Language Course

Plenty of people learn German. Far fewer walk away with a signed contract at a German employer. That gap is exactly what this program is built to close — it’s not just German lessons, it’s German lessons pointed directly at a real job outcome, with the placement support to actually get you there.

What the Placement Process Actually Looks Like

Once your German reaches a working level, our advisors start introducing you to partner hospitals and care facilities directly — real employers, real openings, not a job board you’re left to navigate alone. You’ll get support through the interview itself, then through the contract, the visa paperwork, and the actual move.

How We Prepare You for the Interview, Not Just the Exam

A language exam and a real job interview test different things. We build workplace-specific German into your later lessons — the vocabulary, the small talk, the way a German employer actually phrases questions — so the interview feels familiar instead of like a second, harder exam.

Why Learn With EuroPass

Dr. Rachidi has guided students from Khemisset through this exact process, which means the advice you get is based on what has actually worked, not general guesswork.
