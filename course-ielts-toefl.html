Why This Program

Why a High Score Needs Different Prep Than a Class

A High Score Needs a Different Kind of Prep

Being good at English and being good at IELTS or TOEFL are not the same skill. The exam has its own timing, its own scoring criteria, and its own traps for people who are otherwise fluent. Plenty of strong English speakers walk into the exam unprepared and walk out with a lower score than they expected — not because their English failed them, but because the exam format did.

What Actually Moves Your Score

Small, specific things move an IELTS or TOEFL score more than general practice does: knowing the exact structure examiners are scoring for in Writing Task 2, pacing yourself correctly in the Reading section, and getting comfortable speaking clearly under a strict time limit. These are learnable skills, and they’re what we spend most of our time on.

How We Prepare You, Section by Section

You’ll work through each section — Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking — with strategies built specifically around how it’s scored, then sit full-length timed practice exams so exam day itself holds no surprises. Marc reviews your practice writing and speaking personally, with the same eye an examiner would use.

Why Learn With EuroPass

Marc spent years as an IELTS examiner before joining EuroPass, which means he’s not guessing at what scores well — he’s seen thousands of real responses scored from the other side of the table. That’s a genuinely different level of insight than a teacher who has only taught the exam, never graded it.
